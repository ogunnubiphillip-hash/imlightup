Plan & Prepare

Careful site evaluation and power planning to ensure a smooth setup. We map out the venue's requirements and design a layout tailored specifically to your space before any equipment leaves our doors.

Design & Program

Selecting the right lighting fixtures to complement the venue's natural aesthetics. We plan the visual transitions for every key moment of your event from guest arrival and speeches to dining and the dance floor.
