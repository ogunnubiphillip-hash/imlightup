03 / About Us

Where Technical Skill Meets Creative Design.

Founded by Ademola Benson Ijalana, IM Lightup is dedicated to transforming events through thoughtful lighting design. Based in Ibadan, Nigeria, we specialize in creating beautiful, reliable lighting atmospheres for weddings, corporate gatherings, and private celebrations.

• National Expertise • Professional Event Lighting
Our Core Philosophy

Lighting sets the tone. It transforms the space.

When the sun goes down, lighting is what brings an event to life. It dictates where the eye is drawn, how the room feels, and the energy on the dance floor.

At IM Lightup, we operate at the intersection of technical reliability and creative design. We don't just supply equipment; we carefully plan and operate high-quality lighting setups that elevate your venue, complement your decor, and create the perfect atmosphere for your guests.
